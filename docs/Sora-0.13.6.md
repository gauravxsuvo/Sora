# Sora 0.13.6

- Added **Settings → Animated wallpaper**. Choose a local MP4 or WMV to loop silently behind the Windows desktop icons.
- Sora remembers your video and resumes playback on launch. Enable **Start at sign-in** to start it when you sign in to Windows.
- Select **Turn off wallpaper** to stop playback and clear the saved video.

Exit Sora through its tray menu before installing. Normal upgrades preserve existing profiles and preferences.
